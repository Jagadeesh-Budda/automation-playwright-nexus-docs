## Challenge Exercise

**Business Context**:
You have 45 minutes in a live interview setting for a Senior SDET role at a major pharma company.

**Mission**:
Design and implement a custom retry mechanism for an unstable third-party API that your tests rely on.

**Constraints**:
- Cannot use hardcoded `waitForTimeout`.
- Must implement exponential backoff.

**Deliverables**:
- A robust polling utility function.
- A sample test demonstrating its usage.

**Success Criteria**:
- The utility successfully retries 3 times before failing.
- The implementation proves an advanced understanding of asynchronous JavaScript.

**Hints**:
- Hint 1: Utilize `try/catch` within a loop.
- Hint 2: Use `Promise.resolve` appropriately.
